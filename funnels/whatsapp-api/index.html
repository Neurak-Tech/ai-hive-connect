What exactly am I buying?

Four things: WhatsApp channels (numbers), API keys, a prepaid wallet and webhooks. You build the rest into your own product.

Is this the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. AI Hive is an approved Meta Tech Provider and your number runs on Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform.

Do you take any commission on messages?

No. Your wallet is charged exactly Meta's price per message. Zero commission.

Is the ₹200 price per number?

Yes, ₹200 a month per number plus GST, down from ₹400. Messages are paid separately from your wallet, which recharges from ₹500 + GST.

Can I use it with Cursor, Lovable, Replit and similar tools?

Yes. Anything that can make an HTTP request can call the API, whether you code by hand or build with an AI tool.

Is there an inbox or chatbot builder?

No, on purpose. It's an API, so you only pay for what you integrate.

Do I need a card on Meta?

Never. You recharge your AI Hive wallet in rupees.
